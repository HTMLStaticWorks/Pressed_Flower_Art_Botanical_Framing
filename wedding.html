Our Preservation Process

From bouquet to heirloom: A careful, month-long journey of artistry and preservation.

1. Submit

Book your date and receive our shipping kit. Send your fresh bouquet to our studio within 24-48 hours of your wedding.

2. Press

Our artists carefully deconstruct and press each petal and stem using traditional high-pressure techniques.

3. Compose

Once dried, we recreate the spirit of your bouquet in a custom composition that fits your chosen frame.

4. Frame

Mounted with archival materials and UV-protected glass to ensure the colors stay vibrant for a lifetime.

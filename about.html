Our Story

The passion behind preserving nature's most delicate moments.

Archiving Nature's Beauty

Founded in 2018, Bloom began as a small herbarium project in a sun-drenched studio. Our founder, Elena Thorne, was inspired by the ancient Japanese art of Oshibana and the desire to make the fleeting beauty of a wedding bouquet last forever.

Today, Bloom is a leading botanical studio, blending traditional preservation techniques with modern archival framing to create museum-quality art for homes around the world.

Our Philosophy

The principles that guide our craft, from local organic micro-farms to the final framed masterpiece.

Ethical Sourcing

We partner exclusively with sustainable local micro-farms and licensed gatherers who respect botanical boundaries.

Archival Artistry

Every piece is secured using museum-grade acid-free adhesives, rag mats, and premium UV-protective double glazing.

Sustained Patience

Our slow traditional pressing methods extract moisture gradually over months, safeguarding natural pigment layers.

What We Stand For

Every petal we press is guided by these principles.

Purity

We use only archival, chemical-free pressing methods that honour the natural integrity of every bloom — no dyes, no shortcuts.

Craft

Each piece is hand-finished by our studio team. We believe that in a world of mass production, slowness and attention to detail are radical acts.

Memory

A pressed flower is a moment made permanent. We treat every commission as a privilege — whether it's a wedding bouquet or a wildflower from a favourite walk.

Where the Work Happens

A Studio Built for
Botanical Devotion

Nestled in a light-filled California warehouse, our studio is designed around the rhythm of nature. Large windows flood the pressing tables with soft northern light — the same light that reveals every vein, every texture in a petal.

Our walls are lined with drying racks, specimen drawers, and decades of pressed flowers archived in hand-labelled linen folders. It smells like dried lavender and old books.

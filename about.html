Our Story

The passion behind preserving nature's most delicate moments.

Archiving Nature's Beauty

Founded in 2018, Bloom began as a small herbarium project in a sun-drenched studio. Our founder, Elena Thorne, was inspired by the ancient Japanese art of Oshibana and the desire to make the fleeting beauty of a wedding bouquet last forever.

Today, Bloom is a leading botanical studio, blending traditional preservation techniques with modern archival framing to create museum-quality art for homes around the world.

Studio Milestones

2018

Studio Founding

Bloom opens its first studio space, focusing on local wildflower studies.

2020

Wedding Preservation Launch

We launched our nationwide bouquet preservation service, transforming bridal memories into art.

2022

Workshop Program

Our studio doors opened to the public for hands-on learning in botanical preservation.

How It Works

From a fresh bloom to a framed heirloom — our three-step preservation process.

1

Collect

Choose your blooms at their peak. We guide you on the best specimens for lasting colour and structure.

2

Press

Using traditional high-pressure techniques, each petal and stem is carefully flattened and dried over several weeks.

3

Frame

Mounted on archival paper and sealed behind UV-protective glass, your botanical art is ready to be cherished for generations.

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our botanical supplies.

How long do the dried flowers last?

Our professionally dried flowers can last for years if kept out of direct sunlight and high humidity. We use archival methods to preserve their natural colors as long as possible.

Do you offer international shipping?

Currently, we only ship within the continental US to ensure our delicate products and glass frames arrive in perfect condition without prolonged transit times.

Can I customize a DIY kit?

Yes! We can tailor kits for special occasions or bulk orders. Please contact us through our inquiry form for any custom kit requests.
